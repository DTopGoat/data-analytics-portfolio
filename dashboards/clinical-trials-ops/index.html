Clinical Trials Operations Dashboard — Type 2 Diabetes

Interventional studies registered on ClinicalTrials.gov with a start date between 2015 and 2024 · portfolio status, phase mix, sponsors, site footprint and cycle times

Key findings (all studies, 2015–2024 starts)
  • 4,477 interventional T2D studies planned or enrolled 1.79M participants across 115 countries; 63.1% are completed and 7.0% stopped (terminated, withdrawn or suspended).
  • 19.1% of records have status Unknown (past their expected completion with no update in 2+ years). That's a registry data-quality gap worth flagging to a study-start-up or data-governance team.
  • Phase 3 studies take a median 21.5 months from start to actual completion and enrol a median 320 participants, versus 6.5 months and 34 participants for Phase 1.
  • Industry runs about 30% of studies, led by Novo Nordisk (151) and Eli Lilly (109). The US has 35% of all site records, and China is the #2 country by number of studies.
