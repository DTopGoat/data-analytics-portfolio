E-commerce Sales & Customer KPI Dashboard

UK-based online gift wholesaler/retailer · 1.0M invoice lines, Dec 2009 – Dec 2011 · revenue trends, customer cohorts & retention, top drivers by product and country

Key findings (all markets, full period)
  • Net product revenue was £19.6M from 39,516 orders (AOV £497). Jan–Nov 2011 grew only 3.0% over the same months of 2010, so the business is flat, not growing.
  • Revenue is strongly seasonal. November is the peak month in both years (£1.43M in 2010 and £1.45M in 2011, about 2.3× the spring months), which means stock and staffing should be planned around Sep–Nov.
  • Revenue is concentrated: the top 20% of identified customers generate 77% of revenue, and repeat buyers (72% of customers) account for 97% of it. Retention, not acquisition, drives the P&L.
  • The UK is 85.5% of revenue. EIRE, the Netherlands and Germany are the next biggest markets, at 3.2%, 2.8% and 2.0%.
