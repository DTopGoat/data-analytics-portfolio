Protocol Deviation Dashboard

A clinical operations dashboard designed to give Regulatory Affairs, Quality, and operational leadership a clear view of protocol deviations, documentation gaps, site trends, and deviation timing.

Synthetic / anonymized visualsNo corporate or patient data

What the dashboard answers

  • How many subjects have recorded protocol deviations?
  • Which deviation types and site-error reasons occur most often?
  • Where are protocol questions incomplete or unanswered?
  • How quickly are deviations identified and how long do they remain open?

My role

Requirements analysis, SQL/data preparation, KPI logic, dashboard design, data validation, and presentation to quality and regulatory leadership.

Technology

Looker, SQL, clinical operations analytics, data quality validation
