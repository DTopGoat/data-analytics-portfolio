Study Mapping Application

A centralized application for mapping study and site records across Salesforce, NetSuite environments, and CRIO, with Snowflake maintaining the saved mapping relationships.

Synthetic / anonymized visualsProprietary details omitted

Problem & outcome

Study and site records can use different names, identifiers, and formats across business systems. The application provides one place to compare, match, validate, and maintain those relationships for more consistent downstream reporting.

Key valueLess manual reconciliation · improved data quality · visible mapping status · more consistent reporting

What I built

Data exploration, study mapping, auto-match assistance, legacy mapping, site mapping, mapped-record maintenance, and Snowflake persistence.

Technology

Streamlit, Snowflake/Snowpark, Salesforce, NetSuite, CRIO
