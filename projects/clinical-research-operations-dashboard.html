What Needs Attention

    How it was designed

    Case Study

    This simulation shows how I approach a clinical research BI engagement without publishing confidential operational data.

    Business Questions

    How is recruitment pacing? Which sites are below enrollment target? Are scheduled visits being completed? Where are protocol deviations concentrated? Which data-quality exceptions require operational follow-up?

    Analytics Layer

    Subject-level status logic, visit completion measures, deviation counts and rates, site scorecards, missing-answer monitoring, and reconciliation checks designed as reusable SQL metrics.

    BI Design

    Executive KPIs first, operational trends second, and exception-level detail last. Filters simulate the type of interactive exploration available in Looker while keeping this portfolio site dependency-free.

    Privacy & Security

    The public project uses synthetic values and fictional study/site names. No PHI/PII, employer data, credentials, proprietary SQL, or internal identifiers are committed to this repository.
