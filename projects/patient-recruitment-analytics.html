Patient Recruitment Analytics

An interactive recruitment application for cohort feasibility, protocol pre-screening, patient candidate identification, and site-level matching using clinical and operational criteria.

Synthetic / anonymized visualsPatient information excluded

Problem & outcome

Clinical teams need to evaluate complex protocol criteria without manually reviewing large patient datasets. The application centralizes multi-layer filtering and immediately shows the resulting candidate cohort and site coverage.

Key valueFaster feasibility analysis · consistent filtering · real-time KPIs · exportable candidate cohorts

What I built

Cascading filters for medical conditions, medications, demographics, visits, and laboratory criteria with ANY, Exact Value, and Range modes.

Technology

Snowflake, Snowpark, Python, Pandas, Streamlit
