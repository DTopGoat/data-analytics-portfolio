E-commerce sales and customer KPIs

Revenue, growth, Pareto and cohort retention for a UK online retailer, built from 1.07M raw transaction lines.

Open the interactive dashboard

The question

Leadership at an online gift retailer wants to know whether revenue is growing, where it comes from, and whether customers come back. The raw data is 1.07M transaction lines with cancellations, duplicates and fee codes mixed in.

What I did

I built a cleaned sales layer in DuckDB SQL, wrote KPI, Pareto, year-over-year and monthly cohort-retention queries on top of it, and delivered them as a filterable dashboard in Python and Plotly.

What it shows

  • £19.6M net revenue from 39,516 orders, an average order of £497.
  • Only 3.0% growth from Jan–Nov 2010 to Jan–Nov 2011, with a sharp peak every November.
  • The top 20% of customers drive 77% of revenue, and repeat buyers 97%. The case is for retention and Q4 inventory planning, not acquisition.

Tools

DuckDB, SQL, Python, Plotly
