Report Distribution Automation

Automation pattern

Each workflow starts with a scheduled Snowflake or Looker process, detects a business or data-quality condition, produces an alert/report, and delivers it to stakeholders. Power Automate is used when additional file handling, message formatting, or recipient distribution is required.

Business value

Reduce manual report forwarding, improve visibility into operational exceptions, support timely follow-up, and protect downstream reporting quality.
